The first stable release, and the contract it commits to. The journal is the only authority:
every balance derives from an immutable double-entry ledger, `uncertain` is a first-class answer
rather than an error to hide, business operation keys are strict, and webhook delivery is
at-least-once and honest about it. Simulated money only — no real funds, no real cards, no
production processor routes — and the sandbox-only guards refuse the ways to make it look
otherwise.

Everything on this site documents that release: the `/v1` API with projects and scoped keys, the
hosted checkout and dashboard on their own origins, the operator CLI, the TypeScript SDK, signed
webhooks, the seeded failure-scenario library, and the self-hosting kit — systemd units, the
env-file model, nightly backups and a restore that is drilled, not hoped for.
